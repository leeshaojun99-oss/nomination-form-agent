Nomination Form Agent

Upload the policy PDF → it checks every detail → you approve → it fills the Appointment / Change of Nominee(s) form for every customer. Data never leaves this computer.

1Add a customer from their policy PDF

Upload the customer's policy PDF (for example the ePartner Policy Detail page saved with Ctrl+P → Save as PDF). The app reads what it can and fills the boxes below in green — check them and complete the rest. Then fill parts 2–5 and click Add customer at the bottom. Repeat for more customers.

2Nominees

Paste your WhatsApp nomination details — the table fills automatically — or type them into the table. Put postcode and town inside the address; the app splits them out.

5Consent of existing nominee(s) / trustee(s) form Part 3

Only needed when the current nomination is a trust policy: the existing nominees or trustees must consent to it being revoked. If there are any, paste their details (Name / IC) below — or click Add person and type them.

1…or load a whole Excel list

Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, one row per nominee. A policy with 3 nominees has 3 rows with the same policy number. Start from the blank template so the columns match.

6Review the check results

Red = blocked, will NOT be filled until fixed — correct the details above and add the customer again. Amber = will be filled, but read the note — anything the app wasn't sure about is left blank for you to write in.

7Preview the filled form

Exactly what will be printed, marked PREVIEW. Check page 1–2 against your records.

8Approve & generate

Nothing final is produced until you approve. Only green and amber policies are included.
